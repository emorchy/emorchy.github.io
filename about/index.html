About

Ethan Morchy is a CVE-awarded penetration tester focused on hardware product security, embedded systems, secure code review, and applied security training. His work spans medical-device vulnerability research, hardware protocol analysis, firmware and C/C++ review, applied cryptographic exploitation, reverse engineering, and technical education for security teams.

He is most interested in roles where offensive research meets product security: finding real vulnerabilities, building tooling that makes assessment work repeatable, and translating technical depth into clear reports, training material, and engineering guidance.
